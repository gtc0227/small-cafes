A table for one

Small cafes are built for solo hours. Here is how to use one well.

  • 📖
    Read a chapter or three
    Bring a real book, order something warm, and let the background hum do the rest.
  • ✍️
    Journal or sketch
    One page about your day, or sketch the room. Small cafes are full of small things worth drawing.
  • 💻
    A change-of-scene work session
    An hour of focused work tastes better with a flat white. Buy something every hour or so - that is the deal.
  • 🎧
    Coffee and a podcast
    Window seat, one episode, no screen. Watch the street go by.
  • 🧐
    Try the house special
    Small cafes usually have one drink or bake they are proud of. Ask what it is and order that.
  • 🚶
    Cafe-hop the neighbourhoodWalk it off
    Two or three small cafes, one drink each, a walk in between. A whole afternoon, sorted.

Bring someone

A small cafe table fits two coffees and one long conversation.

  • 💬
    The proper catch-up
    Not a quick call - an actual hour, face to face, phones mostly away. Small cafes are made for this.
  • 🍰
    Split the cake
    Order one slice and two forks, or two slices and no regrets. Either way, share.
  • 🎓
    Study or work date
    Side by side, headphones in, a little company without the pressure to talk the whole time.
  • 🎲
    Cards or a small board game
    A travel-size game fits any table. Keep it quick and keep it friendly.
  • 🗺
    Plan the next thing
    Trips, birthdays, weekends - plans made over coffee somehow actually happen.
  • 😊
    Introduce someone to your favourite
    Everyone has one small cafe they guard like a secret. Share yours with someone who will appreciate it.
